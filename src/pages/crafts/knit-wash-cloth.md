# Knit Wash Cloth

<!-- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XaF5yW4JBX8 -->

## Start

1. [Cast on](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1vm6oaYzHyA&t=330s) 5 stitches
2. Knit all stitches

## Increase

3. Knit 3, yarn over, knit to the end
4. (Repeat step 3 until it's as large as you want)

## Decrease

5. Knit 2, knit 2 together, yarn over, knit 2 together, knit to the end
6. (Repeat step 5 until there are 7 stitches left)
7. Knit 2 togther 3 times

## End

8. BO all stitches
9. Cut yarn and fasten off

# Summary

1. co x5
2. k\*
3. k\*3, yo, k\*
4. ^ to desired size
5. k2, k2tog, yo, k2tog, k\*
6. ^ until 7 left
7. k2tog\*3
8. bo*
9. fasten off
